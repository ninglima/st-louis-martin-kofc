# Fraternal Benefits

One of the unique benefits of membership in the Knights of Columbus is access to world-class fraternal insurance and financial products. The Knights of Columbus maintains the highest possible ratings from A.M. Best (A++ Superior) and Standard & Poor’s (AAA Extremely Strong).

## Available Products

- **Life Insurance** — Affordable term and permanent life insurance for members and their families
- **Annuities** — Retirement income solutions with competitive rates
- **Long-Term Care Insurance** — Protection against the costs of extended care
- **Disability Income Insurance** — Income protection if you cannot work

## Additional Member Benefits

- Scholarships for members and their families
- Orphan benefits for children of deceased members
- Access to the largest Catholic lay organization in the world
