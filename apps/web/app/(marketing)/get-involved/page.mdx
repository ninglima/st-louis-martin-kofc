# Get Involved

Saint Louis Martin Council welcomes Catholics who want to make a difference in their parish and community. There are many ways to serve alongside our brother Knights.

## Become a Knight

Membership is open to practicing Catholic men age 18 and older. Visit [kofc.org/join](https://www.kofc.org/en/join/) and select Council #15256 at St. Theresa’s parish in Ashburn, Virginia. After your application is approved at a monthly business meeting, you will be invited to attend a degree ceremony to complete your membership.

## Volunteer with Us

You do not need to be a member to volunteer at many of our events. We welcome parishioners to help with our monthly food drives, Breakfast with the Knights, and other service initiatives.

## Get More Information

For more information about joining or volunteering, contact Grand Knight Steve Shields at [kofc15256@googlegroups.com](mailto:kofc15256@googlegroups.com).
