# Council Polo Shirt Order

Saint Louis Martin Council polo shirts are available for Brother Knights. Our council shirts feature high-tech sports material with excellent embroidery made especially for our council. The first production run of 28 shirts was delivered with outstanding results.

### Order Information

A second production run requires a minimum of 12 orders. To place an order:

- **By mail:** Complete the order form (PDF) and send with a check
- **By credit card:** Use our Square checkout link for online payment

For the current order form and pricing, or to express your interest in the next production run, contact Grand Knight Steve Shields at [kofc15256@googlegroups.com](mailto:kofc15256@googlegroups.com).
