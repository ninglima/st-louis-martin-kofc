# Become a Knight

Membership in the Knights of Columbus is open to **practicing Catholic men age 18 and older**. Joining connects you to a brotherhood of nearly 2 million Catholic men worldwide dedicated to faith, family, and service.

### How to Join

1. Visit [kofc.org/join](https://www.kofc.org/en/join/) and click “Join.”
2. Select Council #15256 at St. Theresa’s parish in Ashburn, Virginia.
3. Submit your online application — it will be reviewed at the next monthly business meeting.
4. Attend a degree exemplification ceremony to complete your membership.

You can also contact us directly at [kofc15256@googlegroups.com](mailto:kofc15256@googlegroups.com) and a council officer will follow up with you.

### Membership Degrees

As of 2020, the first three degrees have been combined into a single _Exemplification of Charity, Unity and Fraternity_ — an open ceremony that family members are encouraged to attend. The Fourth Degree, focusing on patriotism, is a separate exemplification for those who wish to join the Color Corps or an Assembly.

### Annual Dues

Annual dues are $58, supporting council communications and administration costs. The council can waive dues for any brother or potential brother in financial need.

### No Activity Requirements

There are no set participation requirements. Members choose the projects they join and volunteer only the time they have available, balancing membership with family and work obligations.

### Why Join?

- Strengthen your faith in the company of fellow Catholic men
- Serve your parish and local community through meaningful programs
- Build lasting friendships through regular meetings and events
- Access fraternal insurance and financial benefits for your family
- Participate in a global organization of nearly 2 million members

Ready to answer the call to serve? Contact Grand Knight Steve Shields at [kofc15256@googlegroups.com](mailto:kofc15256@googlegroups.com).
