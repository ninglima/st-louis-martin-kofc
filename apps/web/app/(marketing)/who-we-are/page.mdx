# About Saint Louis Martin Council

The Knights of Columbus is a Catholic men's fraternal society dedicated to supporting those in need. Members and their families offer aid and assistance to the sick, disabled, and needy within the parish and our communities.

Our guiding principle: **In service to ONE, in service to all.**

## Our Four Core Principles

- **Charity** — Serving those in need with generosity and compassion
- **Unity** — Building brotherhood among Catholic men
- **Fraternity** — Supporting one another through fellowship
- **Patriotism** — Honoring God and country through service

## Council History

Saint Louis Martin Council 15256 was established at St. Theresa Catholic Church in Ashburn, Virginia in April 2011. Our local chapter has grown to over 300 members and ranks among Virginia’s fastest-growing councils. Nationally, the Knights of Columbus encompasses approximately 1.9 million members in councils worldwide.

## Community Involvement

Our council focuses on enhancing the quality of life for community members through programs in faith, family, community, and life. We invite you to explore our service programs under Faith in Action or check our upcoming events.
