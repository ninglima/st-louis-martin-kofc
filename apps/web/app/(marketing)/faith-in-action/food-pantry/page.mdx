## Loudoun Food Pantry

_“Feeding the Hungry — Nourishing the Soul”_ (Matthew 25:40)

Saint Louis Martin Council operates a monthly food drive delivering approximately 5,000 pounds of food each month to the Catholic Charities Loudoun Food Pantry in Sterling, VA. During the 2025 fraternal year, the council delivered over 62,000 pounds of food, supporting hundreds of community members experiencing food insecurity.

### Award-Winning Program

Our food drive initiative earned the **2025–26 State Family Program Award** from the Virginia Knights of Columbus, recognizing its exceptional impact and effectiveness.

### How It Works

Each month, food bags are distributed to parishioners at St. Theresa’s Church for pick-up one weekend, then collected the following weekend after Sunday Masses. Volunteers help sort, organize, and deliver the donations to Catholic Charities in Sterling.

### How to Help

- Pick up a food drive bag at St. Theresa’s Church on collection weekend
- Fill it with non-perishable items and return it the following weekend
- Volunteer to help sort and deliver donations

To sign up to volunteer or for more information, contact Grand Knight Steve Shields at [kofc15256@googlegroups.com](mailto:kofc15256@googlegroups.com).
