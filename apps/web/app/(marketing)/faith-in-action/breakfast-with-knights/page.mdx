# Breakfast with the Knights

Join the Knights of Columbus for our monthly pancake breakfast, served after Sunday Masses from September through May. Suggested donation: $10 per adult, $5 per child (children under 3 free).

Each breakfast benefits a different local charity. Come for the food, stay for the fellowship, and help support great causes in our community.

## Mass Times

- After the 7:30am Mass
- After the 8:45am Mass
- After the 10:30am Mass

## 2025–2026 Beneficiary Schedule

- **September 14** — Archdiocese for Military Services
- **October 12** — Agape Pregnancy Resource Center
- **November 9** — Virginia Special Olympics
- **December 7** — Associates of St. John Bosco
- **January 11** — Louverture Cleary School (Haiti)
- **February 15** — Diocesan Work Camp
- **March 15** — St. Theresa School Thunder Appeal
- **May 17** — Foster Care needs
