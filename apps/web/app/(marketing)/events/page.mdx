# Upcoming Events

Saint Louis Martin Council hosts regular meetings, service projects, and social events throughout the year. Below are our upcoming activities.

### Regular Monthly Events

- **Business Meeting** — Third Monday of each month at 7:30pm (Rosary at 7pm beforehand)
- **Monthly Food Drive** — Bag pick-up and drop-off weekends at St. Theresa’s Church
- **Breakfast with the Knights** — Monthly, September through May, after Sunday Masses
