# Faith In Action

Faith In Action is the Knights of Columbus framework for serving our parish, our community, and the Church. Saint Louis Martin Council carries out its mission through programs spanning community outreach, family support, youth development, and pro-life advocacy.

## Our Service Programs

- **Right to Life** — Supporting pro-life pregnancy resource centers throughout Northern Virginia
- **Foster Children Support** — Partnering with Loudoun County to help children in foster care and youth aging out of the system
- **Loudoun Food Pantry** — A monthly food drive delivering thousands of pounds of food to Catholic Charities
- **Breakfast with the Knights** — Monthly breakfast fundraisers after Sunday Masses, supporting a rotating roster of local charities
- **Handy Man Services** — Free home repairs for those who are physically unable to perform the work or cannot afford professional services
- **Honor Flights** — Greeting veterans at Dulles Airport as they return from visiting memorials in Washington, D.C.

To learn more about our service programs or to get involved, contact us at [kofc15256@googlegroups.com](mailto:kofc15256@googlegroups.com).
